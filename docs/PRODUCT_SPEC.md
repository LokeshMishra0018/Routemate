# RouteMate — Product Specification

## 1. Product Summary
RouteMate connects verified college students travelling on the same or compatible routes and around compatible times.

## 2. Core Value Proposition
- **Verified Student Network:** Institutional email verification & college ID document verification.
- **Intelligent Route Compatibility:** Multi-factor matching considering source/destination coordinates, intermediate stops, travel dates, departure windows, transport modes, and travel preferences.
- **Safety First:** Mutual blocking, content moderation, trust scores, verified badges, and audit trails.
- **Cost Sharing & Group Travel:** Shared travel coordination and split-cost estimation without direct payment collection.
